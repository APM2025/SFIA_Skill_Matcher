# SFIA Skill Matcher - User Guide

## What is this app?

The SFIA Skill Matcher helps you identify which professional IT skills you have demonstrated, based on real examples from your work experience. It maps your experiences to the **SFIA framework** (Skills Framework for the Information Age) - an internationally recognized standard used by employers to describe IT and digital skills.

Think of it as a translator: you describe what you did at work, and the app tells you which official SFIA skills match your experience.

---

## Who is this for?

- **Job seekers** wanting to understand which SFIA skills they can claim on their CV
- **Students** preparing for placements or graduate roles
- **Professionals** preparing for performance reviews or career development discussions
- **Anyone** who needs to map their experience to the SFIA framework

---

## How does it work?

The app uses artificial intelligence (specifically, semantic text matching) to compare your written evidence against the official SFIA skill descriptions. It finds the best matches and explains why each skill fits your experience.

### What you need to provide:

You'll describe a specific example from your work using the **STAR format**:

- **Situation**: What was the context? What was happening?
- **Task**: What needed to be done? What was your goal?
- **Action**: What did YOU do? (This is the most important part!)
- **Result**: What was the outcome? What happened because of your actions?

**Plus optionally:**
- **Your level of responsibility**: Describe your role (e.g., "I worked under supervision" or "I led a team of 5 people")

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open the app

Open the app in your web browser. You'll see a form with several text boxes.

### Step 2: Think of a specific work example

Choose ONE specific project, task, or achievement from your work experience. Don't try to describe everything you've ever done - focus on one clear example.

**Good example**: "When I was on placement at Company X, I redesigned their customer database"

**Less good**: "I worked on various databases and did different IT tasks"

### Step 3: Fill in the STAR fields

Break down your example into the four STAR sections:

**Situation** (Optional but recommended)
- Describe the background and context
- Example: *"The company's customer database was slow and caused complaints from the sales team. Data entry errors were common."*

**Task** (Optional but recommended)
- What needed to be done?
- Example: *"My task was to identify problems with the database structure and propose solutions."*

**Action** (REQUIRED - This is the most important!)
- What specific actions did YOU take? Be detailed!
- Example: *"I analyzed the current database schema and identified three tables with redundant data. I created a normalized design following third normal form principles. I wrote SQL scripts to migrate existing data without loss. I documented the new structure and created a user guide for the sales team."*

**Result** (Optional but recommended)
- What was the outcome?
- Example: *"The database query time improved by 60%. Data entry errors dropped by 40% in the first month. The sales team gave positive feedback."*

### Step 4: Add your level of responsibility (Optional)

In the "Level of Responsibility" box, describe your role and how much independence/authority you had:

- *"I worked under close supervision of a senior developer"*
- *"I worked independently but consulted my manager on major decisions"*
- *"I led a team of 3 junior developers and made technical decisions"*
- *"I was responsible for strategic decisions affecting the entire department"*

This helps the app determine your SFIA level (1-7).

### Step 5: Click "Match to SFIA Skills"

The app will process your evidence (this takes a few seconds) and show you:

- **Top 5 matching skills** - The SFIA skills that best match your evidence
- **Match scores** - How confident the app is about each match (shown as a percentage)
- **Relevant evidence** - Which part of your text matches each skill
- **Explanations** - Why each skill was matched to your evidence
- **Detected level** - What SFIA level (1-7) your responsibility description suggests

### Step 6: Review and refine

Look at the top matches. Do they make sense?

- ✅ **If a match looks right**: Great! You can use this on your CV or in applications
- ❌ **If a match seems wrong**: Click "Refine this match" and add a clarification

**Example clarification**: 
*"This was more about testing and quality assurance than software development"*

The app will re-run the matching with your clarification taken into account.

## Understanding Your Results

### Match Scores

Scores are shown as percentages:
- **80%+**: Very strong match - this skill clearly fits your evidence
- **60-79%**: Good match - the skill is relevant
- **40-59%**: Moderate match - some aspects fit, but not perfect
- **Below 40%**: Weak match - probably not the right skill

### SFIA Levels

Each skill can be performed at 7 different levels:

- **Level 1-2**: Follow, Assist - Working under supervision
- **Level 3-4**: Apply, Enable - Working independently, guiding others
- **Level 5-6**: Ensure, Advise, Influence - Leading teams, making significant decisions
- **Level 7**: Set strategy, Inspire - Senior leadership roles

The app will suggest which level matches your responsibility description.

### Skill Codes

Each SFIA skill has a 4-letter code (e.g., "PROG" for Programming). You can use these codes when:
- Filling in job applications that ask for SFIA skills
- Updating your LinkedIn profile
- Discussing skills with recruiters or hiring managers

## Tips for Best Results

### ✅ DO:

- **Be specific**: "I wrote Python scripts to automate data extraction" is better than "I did some coding"
- **Use action verbs**: Designed, implemented, analyzed, created, led, tested
- **Include technical details**: Mention specific tools, technologies, or methodologies
- **Describe YOUR actions**: Focus on what YOU did, not what the team did
- **Give one clear example**: One detailed example is better than vague descriptions of many tasks

### ❌ DON'T:

- **Be vague**: "I helped with various IT tasks"
- **List generic responsibilities**: "Responsible for database management"
- **Mix multiple unrelated examples**: Stick to one cohesive story
- **Use only jargon**: Explain what you did in clear terms
- **Forget the Result**: Always explain what happened because of your actions

## Common Questions

### Q: How many examples should I submit?

**A:** Submit ONE example at a time. If you have multiple experiences demonstrating different skills, run the matcher multiple times with different STAR examples.

### Q: What if I get matches I don't recognize?

**A:** Click on the skill name (if it's a link) to see the full SFIA definition, or look it up on [sfia-online.org](https://sfia-online.org). Not all matches will be relevant - focus on the top 1-3 results.

### Q: Can I use this for my CV?

**A:** Yes! Once you've identified matching skills, you can list them on your CV or profile. For example:

*"Demonstrated SFIA skills: Database Design (DBDS) Level 3, Testing (TEST) Level 2"*

### Q: What if the level seems wrong?

**A:** The level detection is based on your "Level of Responsibility" description. Make sure you clearly describe:
- How much supervision you had
- Whether you led others
- How much decision-making authority you had
- The scope and impact of your work

### Q: The matches seem completely wrong. What should I do?

**A:** Try these approaches:
1. Make your Action section more detailed and specific
2. Use the "Refine" feature to clarify what the work was really about
3. Try a different example that more clearly demonstrates a specific skill
4. Make sure you're describing technical/IT work (SFIA is focused on digital skills)

### Q: Can I save my results?

**A:** Currently, the app doesn't save results automatically. We recommend:
- Taking screenshots of your matches
- Copying and pasting the results into a document
- Keeping notes on which SFIA skills matched which of your experiences

---

## Privacy & Data

- Your evidence text is processed only while you're using the app
- Nothing is saved to a database or sent to third parties
- Once you close the browser, your data is gone
- The app runs the AI matching locally/on the server - your text doesn't go to external AI services

## Need Help?

If you're having trouble getting good matches, consider:

1. **Review the SFIA framework**: Browse [sfia-online.org](https://sfia-online.org) to understand what skills exist
2. **Look at examples**: Search online for "SFIA evidence examples" or "STAR format examples"
3. **Ask for feedback**: Share your STAR examples with a mentor, career advisor, or colleague
4. **Try different examples**: If one piece of evidence doesn't work well, try describing a different project

## Example Walkthrough

Here's a complete example to help you get started:

**Situation**: 
*"My university society's website was built 5 years ago and was difficult to update. Committee members complained that adding news articles required technical knowledge."*

**Task**: 
*"I volunteered to improve the website to make it easier for non-technical users to update content."*

**Action**: 
*"I researched content management systems and selected WordPress based on user-friendliness and budget constraints. I migrated the existing content from static HTML to WordPress, preserving SEO rankings. I created custom page templates matching our branding guidelines. I wrote a step-by-step guide for committee members and delivered a training session for 6 people. I set up automatic backups and security plugins to protect against threats."*

**Result**: 
*"The website update time decreased from 2 hours to 15 minutes per article. Five committee members successfully published content independently within the first month. The website security score improved from 60% to 95% according to online scanning tools."*

**Level of Responsibility**: 
*"I worked independently on this project, making all technical decisions. I consulted with the society president on design choices and user requirements."*

**Expected Matches**: 
- Web Development (WDEV)
- Content Publishing (CNTP)
- User Training (USUP / BURM)
- Information Security (SCTY)

Level: Likely 3-4 (Apply/Enable)
